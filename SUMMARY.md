The model compiles your request into a structured, typed rule (trigger, conditions, actions). A base tier works with no elevated privileges; for system-level actions Argus can use Shizuku. Note: connecting a proprietary provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) is optional — Argus can instead use a self-hosted bridge together with its on-device deterministic actions.
